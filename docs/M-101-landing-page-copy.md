## Voice principles applied

- Names the pain in the first 30 seconds of reading. The candidate is anxious — meet that anxiety honestly.
- No "your French journey," "let's get started," or exclamation points.
- No founder face on hero. Methodology-first credibility.
- Three tiers with explicit time-pressure mapping. The candidate self-selects, not a sales funnel pushing them.
- Refund policy stated honestly, not buried.
- Premium tier (month 2-3 post-launch) shown as waitlist with "Coming soon" — captures email leads.
- Final CTA is "Start the diagnostic" — commits the user to action, not a passive "Sign up."

# Section 7 — FAQ (compressed, only the questions that block conversion)

## EN

**Q: How is this different from PrepMyFrench?**
PrepMyFrench is generalist TEF/TCF prep for all native languages. LeMethodic is built for one specific candidate: an anglophone preparing for the TCF Canada speaking test. The detection of errors, the path, and the feedback are all tuned for English-speaker interference patterns specifically.

**Q: How is this different from Duolingo or Babbel?**
Duolingo and Babbel are designed to teach French to beginners. They work well for that. LeMethodic is designed for B1 learners who already know French but can't perform under exam pressure. Different problem, different product.

**Q: Will this guarantee I pass the TCF?**
The subscription and Sprint don't include outcome guarantees — passing depends on your effort, not just the platform. The Premium tier (coming month 2-3) includes an outcome-based money-back guarantee.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
The diagnostic gives you immediate visibility into your gaps. Real production change takes 4-8 weeks of consistent practice. Candidates ready to commit 5+ hours per week typically see TCF-relevant improvement within 4 weeks.

**Q: Do I need to be at B1 already?**
LeMethodic is designed for B1 → B2. If you're at A2 or below, you'll see this in the diagnostic and we'll redirect you to the waitlist for the A2 → B1 path (in development). If you're already B2+, the diagnostic will tell you and recommend appropriate next steps.

1. **Voice consistency.** Matches the questionnaire copy and cluster docs — direct, names the pain, no hype, no "your French journey."

2. **No founder face on hero.** Per market research: scaled AI products lead with methodology, not biography. Solo-creator products (Clémence, Frederic) lead with face. LeMethodic is positioned as scaled product. Methodology framing wins.

3. **The 7,000 hours mention is in Section 6, not Section 1.** It's supporting credibility, not headline credibility. The headline credibility is "built specifically for the B1 → B2 wall + anglophone interference."
